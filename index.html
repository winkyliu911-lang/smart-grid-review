1.3 Hydropower / 水力发电 Intro 2026 p.29-31

Driving force: The hydrologic cycle (sun evaporates water → clouds → precipitation at high elevation → potential energy → kinetic energy).

驱动力：水循环（太阳蒸发水→云→高海拔降水→势能→动能）。

Pm = η · ρ · g · Q · H
η = turbine efficiency / 涡轮效率, ρ = water density / 水密度 (1000 kg/m³), g = 9.81 m/s², Q = flow rate / 流量 (m³/s), H = effective head / 有效水头 (m)

Best turbine efficiency: 80-90%+. Micro-hydro (<100kW): 60-80% efficient.
最佳涡轮效率：80-90%+。微水力(<100kW)：60-80%。

1.4 Solar Energy / 太阳能 Intro 2026 p.51-58

Solar Thermal / 太阳能热利用 Intro 2026 p.51-53

Extracting heat energy directly from sunlight. Most common: domestic hot water heating.
直接从阳光中提取热能。最常见应用：家用热水加热。

Qhw = Vhc × Cw × Δθ
Qhw = daily heat requirement (MJ), Vhc = daily hot water consumption (m³), Cw = 4.2 MJ/m³/°C, Δθ = temperature difference (°C)

Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) / 聚光太阳能 Intro 2026 p.54-56

Four main technologies: Solar Tower, Parabolic Trough, Parabolic Dish, Compact Linear Fresnel Reflectors.
四种主要技术：太阳塔、抛物线槽、抛物线碟、紧凑型线性菲涅尔反射器。

Process: Reflect sunlight → Concentrate in collector → Heat molten salt → Store → Heat exchanger → Steam engine → Generator.
过程：反射阳光→集热器聚光→加热熔盐→储存→换热器→蒸汽机→发电机。

Photovoltaic (PV) / 光伏 Intro 2026 p.57

Uses semiconductor materials (e.g., Silicon) to generate direct current under sunlight. First PV cell: 1954. Now widely used for rooftop grid-supplementing.

利用半导体材料（如硅）在阳光下产生直流电。第一个PV电池：1954年。现广泛用于屋顶并网。

1.5 Biomass / 生物质能 Intro 2026 p.40-42

Six processing methods:

  1. Direct combustion → power / 直接燃烧→发电
  2. Anaerobic digestion → methane-rich gas / 厌氧消化→富甲烷气体
  3. Fermentation → alcohols / 发酵→醇类
  4. Oil extraction → biodiesel / 油脂提取→生物柴油
  5. Pyrolysis → biochar, gas, oils / 热解→生物炭、气体、油
  6. Gasification → syngas (CO + H₂) / 气化→合成气
Net release of CO₂ is theoretically zero — plants absorb CO₂ during growth, release it during combustion. / 理论上CO₂净释放为零。

1.6 Tidal Power / 潮汐能 Intro 2026 p.43-47

Uses solar and lunar gravitational attraction producing high/low tides twice daily. Works by building a barrage to contain water after high tide; water passes through turbine at low tide.
利用太阳和月球引力每天产生两次高低潮。建造堰坝在涨潮后蓄水；退潮时水通过涡轮发电。

Advantages / 优点

  • Most reliable RE source (not affected by climate change) / 最可靠的可再生能源
  • Unaffected by weather; predictable currents / 不受天气影响，可预测
  • No visual or noise pollution / 无视觉或噪音污染
  • Water is ~400× more powerful than wind / 水的能量密度是风的~400倍

Disadvantages / 缺点

  • Possible effects on sea mammals / 可能影响海洋哺乳动物
  • Very location specific / 非常依赖地理位置
  • Infant technology / 技术仍在发展中

1.7 Geothermal Energy / 地热能 Intro 2026 p.48-50

Comes from hot rocks and liquids produced by volcanic activity. Geothermal heat flow is independent of daily/seasonal influences — no intermittency!

来自火山活动产生的热岩和液体。地热热流不受日/季变化影响——无间歇性！

Advantages / 优点

  • Consistent energy output (no intermittency) / 持续能源输出（无间歇性）
  • Helps stabilize electricity grids / 有助于稳定电网
  • Direct and efficient heating capabilities / 直接高效的供热能力

Disadvantages / 缺点

  • Slower adoption rate / 推广速度较慢
  • High initial investment costs / 初始投资成本高
  • Limited geological data / 地质数据有限
  • Shortage of skilled workforce / 缺乏熟练工人

    PV和风电需要大面积土地（城市地区成本高）

    水力、潮汐、地热取决于当地资源

    限制太阳能获取；需要城市规划保障太阳能使用权

    风力涡轮机噪音、PV眩光
  5. Costs dropping / 成本持续下降 — especially for large-scale applications
    尤其是大规模应用的成本持续下降

2. Wind Power (Key Focus!) / 风力发电（重中之重！）EXAM CRITICAL

The professor emphasized: "We talk so much about wind power... Wind power is VERY IMPORTANT. You need to understand that wind power's mechanical power is proportional to the CUBE of wind speed. Pay attention to the wind power curve."
老师强调：风力发电非常重要！必须理解风力的机械功率与风速的立方成正比。注意风力功率曲线。

2.1 Wind Power Derivation / 风力功率推导 Intro 2026 p.33-36

Wind is derived indirectly from solar energy — generated by air movement from high pressure to low pressure regions established by thermal gradients.
风能间接来源于太阳能——由热梯度建立的高气压区向低气压区的空气运动产生。

风能间接来源于太阳能——由热梯度产生的高低气压区之间的空气运动。

K.E. = ½ × mass × velocity²

Wind volume = A × L, so:

Pw = ½ρAv³

ρ = density of air / 空气密度 (kg/m³), A = swept area of wind blades / 叶片扫掠面积 (m²), v = wind speed / 风速 (m/s)
Critical Concept / 核心概念: Wind power is proportional to the CUBE of wind speed!
风力功率与风速的立方成正比！如果风速翻倍，功率增加8倍！
If wind speed doubles → power increases by 2³ = 8 times!

2.2 Wind Turbine Power Curve / 风力涡轮机功率曲线 Intro 2026 p.37

Three key points on the power curve:
1. Cut-in wind speed (启动风速): Minimum speed to overcome friction and start generating (~3 m/s for modern turbines).
2. Rated output (额定输出): Maximum power output of the turbine.
3. Cut-out wind speed (切出风速): Maximum speed before turbine shuts down for protection from damage.

Between cut-in and rated speed: P ∝ v³. Between rated and cut-out: constant at rated power. Above cut-out: turbine stops.
切入至额定风速之间：P ∝ v³。额定至切出之间：恒功率输出。超过切出风速：涡轮停止。

2.4 Wind Power Fluctuations / 风力波动 Challenges p.28-35

  • Turbulent peak: Seconds to minutes (gusts) / 湍流峰值：秒到分钟级
  • Diurnal peak: Daily wind speed variation / 日变化峰值
  • Synoptic peak: Changing weather patterns (days) / 天气系统变化峰值

Aggregation Effect / 聚合效应 Challenges p.30-35

  • Increasing turbines within a farm → reduces turbulent peak impact (gusts don't hit all turbines simultaneously)
  • Distributing farms over wide geographic area → reduces diurnal and synoptic peaks
  • Single turbine: output = 0 for 10-20% of time. Large farm: output rarely 0, usually between 5-75% of capacity

增加风场内涡轮机数量→减少湍流影响；在广阔地理区域分布风场→减少日变化和天气系统变化影响。

2017 A2: Find the capacity factor for a 500MW generator: runs at 400MW for 3000h, 300MW for 1000h, 330MW for 1500h, shut down for rest of year (8760h).
Solution: Total energy = 400×3000 + 300×1000 + 330×1500 = 1,200,000 + 300,000 + 495,000 = 1,995,000 MWh

4. Demand Response / 需求响应EXAM

The professor emphasized: "For demand response, we have many different types. The most two main types are incentive-based and time-based. Make sure you understand the basics about different types of demand response."
老师强调：需求响应有很多类型。最主要的两种是激励型和分时型。确保理解各类需求响应的基本知识。

4.1 Definition / 定义 Demand Response p.8

US DOE Definition: "Changes in electric usage by end-use consumers from their normal consumption patterns in response to changes in the price of electricity over time, or to incentive payments designed to induce lower electricity use at times of high wholesale market prices or when system reliability is jeopardized."
美国能源部定义：终端用户根据电价变化或激励支付改变正常用电模式，以在批发市场高价时或系统可靠性受威胁时降低用电量。

DR vs DSM: Demand Response (DR) is a subset of Demand Side Management (DSM). DSM is broader (long-term planning); DR is the dynamic/responsive component.

4.2 DR and Electricity Price / 需求响应与电价 Demand Response p.13-14

Without DR: Demand is inelastic (rigid D1) → price = P1 (high, set by most expensive generator).
With DR: Demand becomes elastic (flexible D2) → consumers shift usage → less need for expensive peaking generators → price = P2 (lower).
无DR：需求无弹性→高价。有DR：需求有弹性→消费者转移用电→减少昂贵尖峰发电→低价。

7.1 Definition of Islanding / 孤岛定义 Grid connection p.32

7.3 Anti-islanding in Smart Grid — Key Difference! Intermittency p.50, 53; 6095期末考试重点

CRITICAL DIFFERENCE / 关键区别:
Traditional Grid: Islanding is NOT allowed. Anti-islanding always active. / 传统电网：不允许孤岛，反孤岛始终开启。

PSAI-based Anti-islanding / PSAI反孤岛 Intermittency p.38, 53; Paper-IETPS2017

PSAI = Permissive Signal-based Anti-Islanding
In smart grid operation, the inverter will NOT go into anti-islanding function UNTIL it receives the permissive signal from the Smart Grid. Without the permissive signal, anti-islanding is NOT triggered.

PSAI = 许可信号反孤岛。在智能电网中，逆变器在收到智能电网的许可信号之前，不会进入反孤岛功能。没有许可信号，反孤岛不会触发。

This means:
- When smart grid sends permissive signal → normal anti-islanding operation (disconnect) / 收到许可信号→正常反孤岛（断开）
- When smart grid does NOT send signal → inverter stays connected → intentional island allowed! / 未收到信号→逆变器保持连接→允许有意孤岛！
- This allows the smart grid to dynamically control whether islanding is permitted / 智能电网动态控制是否允许孤岛

13.6 PSAI — Detailed Mechanism / PSAI详细机制 Intermittency p.38, 53; Paper-IETPS2017 p.6

PSAI = Permissive Signal Anti-Islanding (许可信号反孤岛)
Developed under Sandia National Laboratories (US Govt), part of the SEGIS (Solar Energy Grid Integration Systems) project.

How it works:
1. Utility sends a continuous permissive signal via SCADA to the inverter
2. When signal is PRESENT → inverter knows grid is OK → operates normally
3. When signal is ABSENT (grid outage) → inverter has two options:
   a) In traditional mode: activates anti-islanding → disconnects
   b) In smart grid mode: stays connected → intentional island permitted!
4. The smart grid controller decides which mode based on conditions

Advantages of PSAI:
- Facilitates grid support functions without losing islanding detection effectiveness
- Wider voltage/frequency tripping settings → better accommodate utility transients
- Better LVRT performance
- Remote utility control of inverter on/off
PSAI由桑迪亚国家实验室开发。电网通过SCADA发送许可信号。信号存在→正常运行；信号消失→传统模式断开或智能电网模式允许有意孤岛。

13.7 Intentional Islanding — Detailed Process / 有意孤岛详细过程 Intermittency p.54-61

Two Phases of Islanded Operation / 孤岛运行的两个阶段

  1. Transition from grid-connected to islanded / 从并网到孤岛的过渡

    • Protection schemes inside APS and in grid must not be affected
    • APS must have sufficient local generation and energy storage
    • Control system must regulate voltage and frequency within acceptable ranges
  2. Operation isolated from grid / 与电网隔离运行

    • IDAPS manages local supply-demand balance

14.5 Local vs Global Decision Making — Conflict Resolution / 本地vs全局决策冲突解决 DG Smart Grid p.61-62

The Problem / 问题:
- For local decisions: restricted data is acceptable for limited-scope decisions
- For global decisions: data restriction can result in ineffective and potentially harmful decisions
- Centralizing everything is problematic due to excessive communication requirements

The Solution / 解决方案:
1. Modularity / 模块化: Separate the large grid into small independent microgrids → facilitates effective local decision making. Each autonomous unit manages and solves local problems independently.
2. Hierarchical agents / 分层代理: Management agents in every microgrid handle local decisions; conflicts with global priorities resolved through hierarchical coordination.
3. Adjustable autonomy / 可调自主性: Management agents can strategically change autonomy levels of semi-autonomous agents based on current decision model and system state.
4. Intentional islanding / 有意孤岛: During power outage, microgrid can island itself from neighbours to avoid propagation of disruption. Only possible with distributed energy storage backup.
解决方案：模块化将大电网分割为独立微电网→有效本地决策。分层代理架构协调本地与全局。可调自主性让管理代理根据系统状态调整代理自主级别。

  • Balances unpredictable demand/supply on real-time basis using User Agents, DER Agents, Device Agents
  • Demand increases → buy at market price; Demand decreases → sell at market price
  • Supply decreases → buy at market price; DG supply increases → sell at market price
  • Shadow predictor: sends advance warnings when there will be sudden changes in local DG supply

  1. Power rerouting / 电力重路由: When devices or lines fail, reroute power along alternative pathways using network topology models
  2. Resource allocation / 资源分配: Rapidly access new or reserve power supply sources, prioritize combinations by advantages/disadvantages
  3. Dynamic pricing / 动态定价: Shift power sources or limit consumption at prescribed times; negotiate new power supply schedules at reduced cost
Model Answer Framework for IDAPS Questions:
(a) Structure: Smart grid subdivided into modular microgrids (APS). Each APS has MAS with 5 agent types (management, user, DER, device, control) in hierarchical layers. Management agents make high-level decisions; middle-layer agents semi-autonomous.

18.1 Five Common Problems / 五大常见问题 DG Smart Grid p.19-30

Problem / 问题Mechanism / 机制Smart Grid Solution / 智能电网解决方案
1. False/Sympathetic Tripping
误跳闸/同感跳闸		 Healthy feeder B trips for fault on feeder A, because DG on B has high short-circuit contribution and faster OC relay a) Re-set relay settings (faster for non-DG, slower for DG feeder)
b) Add directional element
c) Communication interlock from smart distribution system
2. Reclosing Issues
重合闸问题		 DG must disconnect within 0.2s-2s (anti-islanding). Reclose interval ≥ 2s needed. Instantaneous reclose is NOT compatible with DG! Coordinated reclose with smart grid — communication-based coordination between DG and reclose timing
3. Reduction of Reach
保护范围缩小		 DG infeed reduces current seen by relay → shortens protection zone. Low-current faults near feeder end go undetected. a) Decrease relay minimum pickup current
b) Add line recloser for extended zone
c) Use transformer connection minimizing DG ground fault contribution
4. Voltage Regulation Issues
电压调节问题		 DG provides voltage rise. When DG disconnects (fault), voltage dips for up to 5 minutes. Risk of load equipment damage. a) Install more voltage regulators
b) Allow DG to reconnect more quickly via PSAI-based anti-islanding — only possible with smart grid control
5. Unintentional Islanding
非故意孤岛		 DG continues to power local grid during outage — safety hazard for maintenance workers PSAI-based anti-islanding with remote utility control; in smart grid, can transition to intentional islanding when conditions permit

18.2 Voltage Dips / LVRT / 电压跌落与低电压穿越 DG Smart Grid p.31; Grid connection p.37

Voltage dip is the most common Power Quality (PQ) issue. DG's ability to help depends on technology:
- Large rotating machines: Can support voltage on load side (but limited by transformer impedance)
- Inverter-based DG without LVRT: May worsen the situation — disconnects during voltage dip, reducing support
- Inverter-based DG WITH LVRT: "Rides through" voltage drop; can inject reactive current within milliseconds to help prevent blackouts
电压跌落是最常见PQ问题。无LVRT的逆变器型DG可能加剧问题；有LVRT的可以穿越电压跌落并注入无功电流。
